# Voice profile — «مع الصادقين» (@maalsadiqeen)

This file is read by the `ig-reel` skill. It describes how the page speaks, so any Reel script
written with that skill sounds like this page and not like a generic growth account.

## Who is speaking

A scholarly-religious Arabic page that explains the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt (قرآن، حديث، فقه،
تفسير) with research and reliable sources. The promise is **understanding**, not news, not shock,
not polemics. The audience is educated Arabic speakers, 35–60, in Iraq, Lebanon, the Gulf and the
diaspora — practising or simply curious, tired of shallow and inflammatory religious content.

## Language and register

- Classical Arabic (فصحى التراث). No dialect (ليش/شلون/شنو), no loanwords, no Latin except the
  handle `@maalsadiqeen` and book sigla when unavoidable.
- Full tashkeel on everything sacred or quoted: Qurʾanic verse, ḥadīth, the hero line, the hook.
  Labels and attributions may drop it.
- Honorifics: «عَلَيْهِ السَّلام» on first mention of an Imam, «(ع)» in attributions,
  «صلى الله عليه وآله» for the Prophet.
- Verses: Uthmani spelling inside ﴿ ﴾, sūrah name + Arabic-Indic āyah number.
- Ḥadīth: verbatim, with «— الإمام … (ع) · الكتاب ج١ ص٢٠٨». Never upgrade or invent a grading.

## Rhythm

- On-screen lines: 2–6 words, one idea per line, at most one gold emphasis word (`<em>`) per line.
- The hook asks a real question or names a real tension; it never promises more than the text says.
- Build tension with structure — question → negation («لَيْسَ …») → affirmation («بَلْ …») →
  consequence — not with adjectives.
- Every reel ends with a quiet take-away and a share line («أَرْسِلْهَا لِمَنْ تُحِبّ»), not with
  «تابعونا» or «لايك».

## What this page never does

- No clickbait or fear-selling: «لن تصدق», «صادم», «عاجل», «انتبه قبل فوات الأوان», «!!!».
- No emojis, no exclamation marks, no all-caps, no Latin slogans.
- No naming opponents, no sectarian jabs, no «قالوا/زعموا» contempt, no takfir.
- No claim without a source; no fabricated chain or grading; no verse repaired from memory.
- No promises of worldly results, no numbers used as bait, no «٣ أشياء لا يعرفها أحد».
- No music with a beat under sacred text; no AI faces of holy figures.

## What the audience should feel

Calm, respected, and a little more certain about what the text actually says. If a line would work
on a hype account, rewrite it.

## Practical defaults for `ig-reel`

- Length 30–50 s; the first two seconds carry the question; one idea only.
- Spoken script: 75–110 Arabic words for 40 s at a measured pace (slower than English).
- On-screen text is the primary channel; assume sound-off viewing.
- CTA: «أَرْسِلْهَا لِمَنْ تُحِبّ» or a named recipient («أَرْسِلْهَا لِصَدِيقٍ يَسْأَلُ …»), rotating.
